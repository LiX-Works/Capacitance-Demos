# Controls / 2026-10-23 English edition

On screen: each page loops its animation. Drag the θ slider to take over the angle (the loop stops); the round ▶ / ❚❚ button plays or pauses. On page 04 a three-way switch shows field lines, potential or field strength; after each completed loop that page also steps through the three views by itself. Drag the 3-D model to turn it; a Reset view button appears once it has moved. Previous / Next turn pages. Contents and Methods sit in the header.

Keyboard: Left / Right turn pages and Right interrupts the loop; Space plays or pauses; R replays; P toggles autoplay through all pages; G opens the contents; T folds the text; Home / End jump to the first / last page; Escape closes dialogs. E enters Explore, which adds a free camera, wheel zoom, the full-width (true proportion) view and the quality setting.

The earlier 12-slide version, its dielectric toggle, preset-angle buttons and log-axis button were removed in this edition; see the collection CHANGELOG.
