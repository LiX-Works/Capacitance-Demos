Background and limits

Both demos began as two talks in an undergraduate sensors course. The course asked for the principles, usually shown on slides; these talks were built as interactive web demos instead, ProxiTouch first and the numerical lab second.

ProxiTouch is a concept design; it has not been fabricated or measured. The lab shows numerical results with explicit parameters and limits; the browser reads precomputed data and interpolates between solved angles. Neither presents animation or simulation as experimental performance.

The 3-D views need a WebGL2 browser. Full source, data and reproduction notes ship with the repository, and both demos also open offline.
