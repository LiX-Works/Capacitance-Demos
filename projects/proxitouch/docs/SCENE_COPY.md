# ProxiTouch / current scene text

Exported from the built scene definitions of the 2026-10-25 English presentation edition. The original Chinese upload remains unchanged in the reference archive.

## S00 / ProxiTouch

**One pixel. Three stages.**

One shared electrode. Two sensing channels.

## S08 / Sense before contact

Unfold the plates → **the field reaches into air**.

A nearby hand changes **mutual capacitance**.

\[\boxed{S_{\mathrm{prox}}=\left|\frac{\Delta C_m}{C_{m0}}\right|}\]

## S09 / An ionic interface

Ions form **electric double layers** at the electrodes.

Two interfaces act in series:

\[\frac{1}{C}\approx\frac{1}{C_{\mathrm{top}}}+\frac{1}{C_{\mathrm{bottom}}}\]

**Charge separation is interfacial.** The bulk is screened.

## S11 / More pressure, more contact

The flexible electrode rests on **micro-domes**.

Press → domes flatten → **contact area grows**.

\[\boxed{P\uparrow\Rightarrow A_{\mathrm{eff}}\uparrow\Rightarrow C_{\mathrm{EDL}}\uparrow}\]

> Gold patches show contact.

## S12 / Two senses, one pixel?

**Proximity** · a field in the air

**Pressure** · an ionic interface

**Can one pixel do both?**

## S13 / One shared electrode

The flexible centre electrode **\(E_C\) links both channels**.

Before touch · fringe-field sensing

After touch · the upper ionic interface

\[\boxed{E_H\leftrightarrow E_C\;\longrightarrow\;E_C\leftrightarrow E_B}\]

## S14 / Inside one pixel

**\(E_H\)** · frame electrode

**\(E_C\)** · flexible, shared electrode

**\(E_B\)** · bottom electrode

**Ionic-gel micro-domes**
A thin air gap lies above.

## S19 / One touch, two channels

**Approach** · \(E_H\leftrightarrow E_C\)
Proximity rises before contact.

**Touch** · the hand-over
Both channels respond.

**Pressure** · \(E_C\leftrightarrow E_B\)
Contact area and EDL capacitance grow.

## S18 / Read the channels in turn

**Time-division multiplexing**

HC · \(E_H\leftrightarrow E_C\)
CB · \(E_C\leftrightarrow E_B\)

\[HC\rightarrow CB\rightarrow HC\rightarrow\cdots\]

Fast switching makes the readout **appear continuous**.

## S20 / Pixels become a surface

Square pixels tile into an array.

Neighbours **share frame edges**.

Scan to address **one pixel at a time**.

## S21 / Two maps, one surface

Before touch → **proximity map**

After touch → **pressure map**

**Where it nears. Where it lands. How hard it presses.**

## S22 / ProxiTouch

Senses before contact.
Registers the first touch.
Reads local pressure.

**Approach → Touch → Press**

> Application concept · not experimentally validated
